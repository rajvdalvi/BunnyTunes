### 🎉 What's New in BunnyTunes V1:
- **Liquid Glass by Default:** Deep OLED glass theme with ambient background glow enabled out of the box.
- **Apple Music Aesthetic:** Fresh new launcher icon featuring liquid gradient sweeps and stylized bunny studio headphones artwork.
- **Top-Left Home Branding:** Pinned "Bunny Tunes" logo directly onto the top-left navigation header.
- **Custom BunnyTunes Emblems:** Replaced old emblems with transparent vector BunnyTunes logos across album arts ("Top picks for you", "Find your mood") and library cards.
- **In-App Themes & Changable Icons:** Added custom vector theme icons including Neon Violet, Neon Cyan, Neon Silver, and Monochrome adaptive icon packs.
- **History in Navbar:** Replaced legacy radio/similar mix menus with direct, intuitive access to your listening history right in the navigation dock.
- **Hindi & Indian Music Feed:** Default region locked to India (`IN`), instantly loading trending Bollywood hits, Indian charts, and regional playlists.
- **Lossless Audio Quality:** Enforced highest bitrate audio decoding across Wi-Fi and mobile data with bit-perfect DAC pass-through support.
- **Cleaned & Stripped:** Completely stripped background Wear OS services and wear-sdk daemons, optimizing battery and reducing memory usage.
- **Settings Rebranded:** Customized app settings with "BunnyTunes V1", developer links to Raj Dalvi's GitHub, and the live showcase website.
- **Signed & Verified:** Packaged with APK Signature Scheme v2 and v3 for full Android 14/15/16/17 compatibility.
